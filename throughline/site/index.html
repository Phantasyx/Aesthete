Your pipes

How the line works

Each player joins two fixed ends. The source opens toward the board, and the gauge opens back toward the pipes. The pieces are caps, elbows, straights, and tees. A finished line has no bare end. That closed path is the throughline. If a pipe runs off the board, that opening can never be closed, and the line cannot be finished.

Why it is here

PhantasyX builds custom software. Throughline is a small example of a shared session: two browsers keep one board, one turn at a time, and the score is the same on both screens. Solo play stays on this page if you want to practice the puzzle alone.

The score

The tray starts with every pipe the perfect fill needs. Lay more of them and the score climbs. Scrap one and the ceiling drops. A direct run to the gauge still seals. It just banks a smaller number. Chasing the full side is the risk, because a pipe that opens the wrong way can leave an end you can never close.
